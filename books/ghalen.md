This book was really good, I’m still going back and forth if I rate it 4 stars or 3 but nonetheless I really enjoyed this one. I finished it in all but 3 days tops. This was a crazy experience for me, somewhat out-of-body if you will. 

Reason being, I really don’t read these types of books. These coming of age, and literary fiction novels. What’s funny is throughout the entire first act I’m sitting there waiting for the crime to happen—though there did end up being crime—this novel was just so much different than what I’m used to reading. 

I really enjoyed the Ghalen story. 

I wold not catogorize this as a romance at all (though upon doing research this book follows a more traditional romance approach. Romance is a genre that has changed its definition, now seen as strictly romantic relaitonships, romance used to mean the exploration of any relationship)

Walter Mosely is simply a writer I enjoy reading. His stories engage me every time.

Can’t wait to read the next one.
