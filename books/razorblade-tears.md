People LOVE this book. And it’s not bad. . .but it doesn’t match the hype levels of it.

Because I read All The Sinners Bleed first I feel like I can see the improvements in S.A. Cosby’s writing and storytelling. The story for this one was solid, but it felt like it remained on the surface level. The discussions around gayness, and homophobia felt like something you’d hear in an early 2000’s movie.

I’m writing this review later than when I finished it so some of the things are a bit hazy. One of the things I remember are the action scenes being weak. Felt like pointless paragraphs of prose that didn’t at all feel engaging.
