Dope book. Ending (last3-4 chapters) had a lot of twist I didn’t see coming at all. The way Taylor Adams writes his characters is super engaging. The battle between his protagonist and antagonist are really enjoyable. Though this one doesn’t live up to the heights of No Exit—admintingly a tough act to follow—it still is a clear strong suit of his. 

The middle dragged a little bit. Specifically the battle between Lena & Ray, I just didn’t *feel* it. I don’t have too much analysis yet on that part, besides the feeling not quite landing for me. 

It was like Ray and Lena were engaging in this battle but it felt like we missed a scene. There were parts where they talked like they knew each other more than they did, when in reality they don’t know each other.
