Unfortunately this book was trash. I like Marc, and I’m rooting for his success. I will not be reading anymore of this series but if he writes another standalone or series down the line I would for sure check it out. But *this.* This was terrible. 

The characters were extremely flat. Everyone sounds the same. The writing in general was flat. The metaphors were over done and weak throughout. The entire plot was terrible and not emotionally gripping to me at all. 

Discipline is not witty at all, he's just walking up and shooting people. I don't understand why the officers didn't zone in on the sole suspect hearing "sure she knows me, we go way back." Instead they go off the idea that they have no leads. And then the lawyer is taking $100k via CASH APP. (This is a crooked police officer and lawyer bytheway.) 

I cannot stress this enough that Discipline is literally going around shooting people freshly out of prison and nobody can catch him. These must be the types of officers that are in Gotham. Pure incompetence and stupidity. 

This is one of the worst books I’ve ever completed. If I wasn’t trying to read 52 books this year I would have chucked this into the pits of the ocean. Instead I’m left contemplating blowing my head off for suffering through this.
