This book was solid. Not great but definitely not a snooze fest. I really enjoy the writing of Walter Mosley. Mainly his dialogue—which is two fold for me. On one end I absolutely despise his use of dialect. It is an absolute pain to read and I hate with every fiber of my being. That aside, in every one of his novels I enjoy the conversations he creates. Dialogue between his characters are super fun. The only other downside being that there’s a tad too many characters. It was a bit difficult to keep up with who was who and what was happening at certain points. 

Overall the plot was solid and I look forward to continuing this series. Easy is really enjoyable to read. He’s not the most loveable, he does things you may hate—like sleeping with his “friends” wife and planning to move them to Mexico—yet he’s enjoyable to read. I like him as a character, especially because I’ve read Blood Grove, I’ve gotten to see a bit of his growth and where he ends up. 

This wasn’t the peak of mystery writing by any means but it was a fun read and I can’t wait to delve in further with this series.
